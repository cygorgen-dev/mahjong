Cantonese Mahjong 廣東麻雀 — Rules

◀ Close Rules

Overview

Hong Kong Cantonese Mahjong is played with 144 tiles by 4 players. Each player aims to build a winning hand of 4 sets + 1 pair (or a special hand) worth at least the Min Faan threshold (default: 2 faan).

Wins are paid in points calculated from faan. The discarder pays the winner for everyone (discard win); or all three opponents pay equally (self-draw). Payouts are capped at Max Faan (default: 7 faan).

Sets

SetChineseDescription
Chow上3 consecutive tiles of the same suit (e.g. 3–4–5 Bamboo). Can only be claimed from the player to your left.
Pung碰3 identical tiles. Can be claimed from any player.
Kong槓4 identical tiles. Draw a replacement tile from the wall after declaring. An existing Pung can be upgraded to Kong by drawing the 4th tile — opponents can then rob it (搶槓胡) to win.

Winning

A winning hand requires 4 sets + 1 pair worth at least Min Faan, or a recognised special hand (Seven Pairs, Thirteen Orphans, or a 7/8 bonus-tile win).

Discard win 出銃: the discarder pays the winner the full amount (base × 4). Other players pay nothing.
Self-draw 自摸: all three opponents each pay the winner (base × 2).

Flowers & Seasons

Each of the 8 bonus tiles has an index 0–3 corresponding to seats East / South / West / North. When a bonus tile's index matches your current seat wind, it is your own flower or season and scores 1 faan. Having all 4 Flowers or all 4 Seasons scores an extra +1 faan (the set bonus stacks with the own-tile match).

Demo & Scenario Testing

The header bar includes two developer tools for verifying game behaviour:

  • Demo Left / Right / Top / You 💡 — injects a pre-built winning hand for each seat in turn. Also cycles through dealer-is-winner variants (shown with 莊 suffix) to check that the seat label, wind tile, and dice display correctly when the dealer wins.
  • Demo Rob 搶槓 — cycles through four Robbing the Kong scenarios so you can verify that the correct players are offered the Win button.

For more detailed testing, open scenario.html in a second tab. It lets you inject an arbitrary hand, melds, bonus tiles, and discard tile into the live game and verify which claim buttons (Win / Pung / Kong / Chow) are enabled — without having to play through a full hand to reach the desired state.
